Selected work

  • Post-quantum TLS measurement

    Work I led at Vanderbilt's Institute of National Security: a census of post-quantum TLS across 33,069 government, university, and financial domains, plus a 1M-site extension. A Go prober and a statistics layer (Wilson intervals, Manski bounds) found 3,834 domains whose homepage uses ML-KEM while their login, checkout, or API path is still classical-only.

  • vectr

    case study · live · pitch

    Pre-arrival spatial intelligence for EMTs: scene intel, routing, and helicopter landing-zone scoring. Rust and PostGIS backend, field-tested on a 3-day ride-along with Greene County EMS.

  • HUSH-MESH

    code · pitch

    Convoy-protection simulation: an A* mission planner over MQTT edge agents, with a React view of the convoy, drones, and threats. Rewritten after the hackathon to fix a cross-thread bug that dropped every planner recommendation.

    2nd Place ($15K), Amazon Mission Autonomy Hackathon

  • Sentinel

    LLM safety study at the Institute, where I built the probe pipeline: 4,472 harmful-request probes across 5 models behind 0 to 90% many-shot padding, with Wilson intervals per cell. Refusal fell by up to 22.7 points.
